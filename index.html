Publication

2025, International Journal of Science and Business, 48(1), 1–13

Does Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) Affect Financial Performance and Market Value? Evidence from the Banking Industry of Bangladesh

Anik Mitra and A. A. Hamid

Using bank-level secondary data from the Bangladeshi banking sector, the study regresses profitability (ROE) and market valuation (Tobin's Q) on CSR spending with standard firm-level controls.
